Benefits A–Z: free numbers and guides

Not sure which number to ring? This page lists the main benefits people ask us about, with the free official number for each and a link to our plain-English guide. Every number below starts 0800 and is free from mobiles and landlines. Relay UK users dial 18001 first.

Universal Credit

A monthly payment for people on a low income or out of work, including help with rent, children and childcare.

LineNumberOpening hours
Universal Credit helpline0800 328 5644Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm
Universal Credit (Welsh)0800 328 1744Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm
Northern Ireland Universal Credit Service Centre0800 012 1331See nidirect

Guides: claiming · questions and answers · payments · housing costs · childcare · sanctions · advances.

Personal Independence Payment (PIP)

For people aged 16 to State Pension age with a long-term condition that affects daily living or getting around. Not means-tested.

LineNumberOpening hours
PIP new claims0800 917 2222Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm
PIP enquiry line (existing claims)0800 121 4433Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm

Guides: starting a PIP claim · PIP enquiries.

Disability Living Allowance (DLA)

Now only for new claims for children under 16 in England and Wales; some adults still receive it.

LineNumberOpening hours
DLA helpline (children; adults born after 8 April 1948)0800 121 4600Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm
DLA (adults born on or before 8 April 1948)0800 731 0122Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm

Guide: DLA explained.

Attendance Allowance

For people over State Pension age who need help with personal care because of an illness or disability.

LineNumberOpening hours
Attendance Allowance helpline0800 731 0122Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm

Carer's Allowance

£86.45 a week if you care for someone for at least 35 hours a week and they get a qualifying disability benefit. Universal Credit claimants may also get the carer element.

LineNumberOpening hours
Carer's Allowance Unit0800 731 0297Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm

Budgeting Loans

Interest-free loans for people who have been on certain older benefits for six months. Universal Credit claimants get a Budgeting Advance instead.

LineNumberOpening hours
Social Fund Enquiry Line0800 169 0140Mon–Fri, 10am–3pm
Social Fund (Welsh)0800 169 0240Mon–Fri, 10am–3pm

Guides: Budgeting Loans · Budgeting Loan number.

New Style JSA and ESA

Contribution-based benefits that depend on your National Insurance record, which you can get alongside or instead of Universal Credit.

LineNumberOpening hours
New Style JSA / ESA new claims0800 055 6688Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm
Jobcentre Plus (existing ESA/JSA claims)0800 169 0310Mon–Fri, 8am–5pm

Challenging a decision

For any of these benefits, you can ask for a mandatory reconsideration and then appeal to an independent tribunal. See how to appeal. To complain about service, see complaints.

Check what you could get

Free, anonymous benefits calculators on GOV.UK (and Turn2us) show what you might be entitled to. Our UK benefits by situation page helps you work out where to start.

You never need to pay to contact DWP. Ignore websites offering 084, 087 or 09 "connection" numbers for benefits.
