Video help and accessible ways to contact Universal Credit

Phoning a helpline is not easy for everyone. If you are deaf, hard of hearing, have a speech impairment, find reading difficult or prefer Welsh, there are official ways to get the same help. This page brings them together.

British Sign Language (BSL) video relay

DWP runs a free video relay service so BSL users can talk to Universal Credit and other DWP lines through an interpreter on a video call. You can use it on a computer, or on a mobile or tablet. Start it from the DWP video relay service link, which GOV.UK lists on its Universal Credit contact page.

Relay UK (text relay)

If you cannot hear or speak on the phone, dial 18001 before the number to connect through Relay UK. For example:

LineRelay UK
Universal Credit18001 then 0800 328 5644
PIP new claims18001 then 0800 917 2222
PIP enquiry line18001 then 0800 121 4433
DLA helpline18001 then 0800 121 4600
Social Fund (Budgeting Loans)18001 then 0800 169 0140

Find out how Relay UK works, including the app, on the Relay UK website.

Easy read and other formats

GOV.UK publishes an easy read guide to Universal Credit, and several benefit guides (including PIP, Budgeting Loans and DLA for children) are available in Welsh, easy read or BSL. If you need letters in another format, such as large print or braille, ask DWP through your journal or the helpline.

Video guides

GOV.UK links to an official video showing how to use the Universal Credit service on a mobile or tablet. It is a good way to see the screens before you start a claim. Our written walk-throughs: claiming Universal Credit and using your journal and account.

Someone to help you

  • A friend or relative can join your call, or call on your behalf if you are with them.
  • Citizens Advice Help to Claim can support you with a new claim: England 0800 144 8 444, Scotland 0800 023 2581, Wales 08000 241 220, Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, plus web chat.
  • You may be able to get a home visit for a PIP claim — ask when you call.

Can't manage your claim online?

Call the Universal Credit helpline on 0800 328 5644 straight away if you cannot manage online and need to claim by phone, or cannot respond to messages in your account. Telling DWP early helps you avoid missed payments or sanctions.
